04 / BEHIND THE WORK

A little about me.

I’m studying Creative Technology & Design at CU Boulder and drawn to projects that move between disciplines. One day I’m prototyping electronics; the next I’m building scale models, refining a visual identity, or planning a digital product.

My work also extends into audio production, including original scoring and sound design for moving-image projects. I enjoy creating experiences that feel cohesive across visuals, interaction, and sound.
